# Prior art and credit

On 2026-11-13, `https://oeis.org/search?q=id:A004137&fmt=text` returned one entry, containing the literal wording and the published a(14)=68. The reviewed answer identifies Peter Luschny's A193802 example (2011) as the source of the non-Wichmann witness; this is not a new discovery. It cites B. Wichmann, *A note on restricted difference bases*, Journal of the London Mathematical Society 38 (1963), 465–466, for the construction; Arch D. Robison, *Parallel Computation of Sparse Rulers* (2014), for exhaustive computation; Fabian Schwartau, Yannic Schröder, Lars Wolf and Joerg Schoebel, *Large Minimum Redundancy Linear Arrays*, IEEE Open Journal of Antennas and Propagation 2 (2021), 79–85, for computations through length 244; Ed Pegg Jr. (2020), Wolfram Blog, for a sparse-ruler excess bound; and Aleksi Saarela and Aleksi Vanhatalo (2026), Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 33(1), P1.40, for sparse rulers and partial words. These descriptions follow the answer and were not independently re-read here. The weaker optimal-length conjecture is not settled by the witness.
